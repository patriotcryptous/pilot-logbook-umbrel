How to set it up (3 minutes)
  1. Install the free ntfy app on your phone (App Store or Google Play) and allow notifications.
  2. In ntfy, tap + to subscribe to a topic and enter this private name: {{ (al.url or suggested).rsplit("/", 1)[-1] }}. Keep the server as ntfy.sh.
  3. Paste {{ al.url or suggested }} into Send alerts to below (it's filled in for you), tick Send expiry alerts and click Save alerts.
  4. Click Send a test alert. Your phone should buzz within a few seconds.

Alerts cover your medical and flight review (enter both under Pilot above), endorsements with an expiry, Hangar documents with an expiry date, and your passenger currency. The name is the only thing protecting your topic, so keep it long and random.
