Bundled reference data, used offline:
- airports.csv.gz: airportsdata (MIT, see LICENSE-airportsdata.txt), trimmed to the columns the app uses.
- runways.csv.gz: OurAirports runways (public domain, https://ourairports.com/data/), open runways only.
- us-states-arcs.json, us-counties-arcs.json.gz: us-atlas (ISC, see LICENSE-us-atlas.txt), US Census Bureau boundaries.
